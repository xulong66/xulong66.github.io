# Patents Section Design

## Goal

Add a concise English-language Patents section to Long Xu's existing academic homepage using four verified Chinese patent records.

## Content

Display the records in reverse chronological order by publication or grant date:

1. `CN121368013B` — granted on May 1, 2026.
2. `CN119938160A` — published on May 6, 2025; application number `202411778024.X`.
3. `CN117880887A` — published on April 12, 2024.
4. `CN116209103B` — granted on February 27, 2024.

Each record shows an editorially polished English title, inventors with **Long Xu** emphasized, assignee, stable status wording, date, patent or publication number, and an English Google Patents link.

## Experience

- Add `Patents` to the primary navigation between `Publications` and `Education`.
- Place the Patents section after Selected Publications.
- Preserve the site's established editorial academic visual system.
- Use compact patent cards with a patent icon, status badge, year, metadata, and an external `Patent` link.
- Renumber Education and Contact to keep the section sequence continuous.
- Keep the section responsive and accessible at the existing breakpoints.

## Constraints

- All visible homepage copy remains in English.
- Do not add speculative claims or unverified legal-status language.
- Use `Granted Chinese Invention Patent` for `B` records and `Published Chinese Patent Application` for `A` records.
- Preserve the existing social preview and metadata because the site's branding is unchanged.
- Publish through the repository's existing GitHub Pages workflow to `https://xulong66.github.io/`.
